Hinterland Browser

User guide

Everything you need to install, customize, and use Hinterland comfortably. This guide is written for everyday use and does not require technical knowledge.

1. Get started

Install Hinterland

  1. Open the download page and choose the Windows download.
  2. Open the downloaded installer and follow the on-screen steps.
  3. Start Hinterland from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.

Only download Hinterland from the official website or its official release page. If your operating system shows a confirmation prompt, check that the application name is Hinterland before continuing.

Your first window

Use the address bar to enter a website or search phrase. Open a new tab with the + button or Ctrl + L to focus the address bar. Close the current tab with Ctrl + W. Shortcuts can be changed in Settings.

2. Browse comfortably

Tabs and pinned tabs

Each tab keeps a page open without replacing the others. Pin a frequently used tab from its tab menu; pinned tabs take less space and remain available when you reopen the browser. Drag tabs to reorder them or use the tab menu to move them into a group.

Private tabs

A private tab is useful when you do not want local browsing history for that session. Private browsing does not make you anonymous to websites, your network, or your internet provider. Close private tabs when finished.

Reader mode and media

When a page supports it, Reader Mode removes distractions and presents the main article in a simpler layout. The media controls let you pause or resume supported audio and video without searching through tabs. Picture-in-picture keeps supported video visible while you work in another tab.

Search quickly

Enter a search phrase in the address bar. You can choose your preferred search provider in Settings. A keyword can send a query directly to a selected provider when that feature is enabled.

3. Privacy and permissions

Protection by default

Hinterland includes tracker blocking, HTTPS-First navigation, Do Not Track, and referrer controls. Some pages may look different or fail to load when a site depends on third-party content. If a trusted page breaks, use its site controls rather than disabling protections everywhere.

Site permissions

When a site requests access to features such as notifications, location, camera, microphone, or clipboard, review the request before choosing Allow or Block. You can change a decision for the current site in the site-permissions panel. Prefer asking each site when needed instead of granting broad access.

Cookies and saved data

Deleting site data can sign you out and remove preferences for that site. Before clearing data, make sure you know how you will sign in again. Do not save sensitive information on a shared computer.

Important: Privacy features reduce tracking in the browser, but they cannot protect information you deliberately submit to a website. Check the address and the site’s identity before entering passwords, payment details, or personal information.

4. Organize your work

Tab Stash

Use Tab Stash to put tabs aside without losing their addresses. Give a stash a clear name, reopen individual pages when needed, or reopen the whole stash. Remove entries you no longer need to keep the list manageable.

Reading List

Save pages you want to read later. Add a short note when the reason matters, then mark the item complete or remove it after reading.

Quick Notes

Quick Notes are for temporary thoughts connected to your browsing. Treat them as ordinary local notes: review them periodically and avoid putting passwords, recovery codes, or other secrets in them.

Zoom

Adjust the page zoom from the browser controls. Hinterland remembers zoom choices per site so a difficult-to-read site can stay comfortable without changing every other page.

5. Settings and extensions

Customize safely

Open Settings to change appearance, startup behavior, search, privacy defaults, shortcuts, downloads, and site permissions. If a setting has a per-site exception, that exception takes priority for the selected site.

Extensions

Install extensions only from sources you trust. Review the permissions shown before enabling one. Disable or remove extensions you no longer use. An extension may be able to read or change pages depending on the access it requests.

Passwords

The password manager stores credentials in an encrypted vault protected by a password you choose. Use a strong, unique vault password and keep a safe recovery plan. If you forget it, encrypted data may not be recoverable. Never share your vault password, recovery material, or unlocked device.

Sync

If sync is available in your version, enable it only on devices you control. Use a strong account password and sign out on shared devices. Sync may include the categories you choose, such as settings or saved items; review the selection before enabling it. You can turn sync off and remove the account from Settings.

6. Troubleshooting

A page will not load

  • Check the address and your connection.
  • Reload the page and try a new tab.
  • Review the site-permissions panel and privacy controls.
  • Temporarily test in a private tab to identify whether local site data is involved.

Hinterland feels slow

Close unused tabs, pause heavy media, and disable extensions you do not need. Restart the browser after large sessions. Keep the application up to date using the official download page.

Something looks wrong

Record what you expected, what happened, the page address without private query details, and the steps that reproduce it. Do not include passwords, tokens, private notes, or personal browsing history. Send a concise report through the feedback form or the official issue page.

Resetting your setup

Before resetting browser data, export or write down anything you intentionally want to keep. Resetting can remove tabs, saved items, permissions, preferences, or vault data depending on the option selected. When uncertain, ask for help before confirming.

Safety rule: Support will never need your password, vault password, one-time code, or private browsing history. Keep those details private.

7. Accessibility and responsible use

Use system scaling, browser zoom, keyboard shortcuts, and reduced-motion preferences to make browsing comfortable. Keep focus visible and use headings and controls in their normal order. Hinterland is intended for lawful, respectful browsing; follow each website’s rules and protect other people’s information.

For release notes and downloads, visit the official website. For feedback, use the link in the site footer.
